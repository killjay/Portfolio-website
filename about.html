(01)   About

Designer, not decorator.

The best design gets out of the way. You only notice it when something hard finally feels easy.

Hey, I'm Nandha. I'm a product designer in the Bay Area. I've been doing this since 2019, mostly some mix of UX research, strategy, and design, with the goal of building things that just make sense.

Most of my work has been on AI and immersive tech products. The part I like is figuring out how something complicated can feel simple once you actually use it. A lot of that is just being curious about how people use the thing.

Outside of work, I'm usually behind a camera or watching a film I've already seen too many times. Both are good reminders that the story is what people remember.
